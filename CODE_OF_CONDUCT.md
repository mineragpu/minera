# Code of conduct

Minera is built in the open by people with different backgrounds, languages and levels of
experience. This page sets the standard for how we treat each other in the repository's issues,
pull requests, discussions and reviews, and in any other space run by the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers and people writing in a second language.
- Keep feedback about the work. Say what is wrong, why, and what would fix it.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you conclude.
- Accept review gracefully, and credit the ideas and work of others.
- Keep discussions on topic, and move long side threads to their own issue.
- Respect people's privacy and time.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats or intimidation of any kind.
- Discriminatory language or jokes, and unwelcome sexual attention or content.
- Personal attacks, trolling and deliberately derailing a discussion.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as a home address, wallet ownership or contact
  details, without their explicit permission.
- Promoting tokens, trading or other projects, spam, and impersonating the maintainers.
- Disclosing a vulnerability in public before it is fixed. Security issues follow
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

The maintainers enforce this code. They may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other
contributions that break it, and may warn, temporarily restrict or permanently ban anyone whose
behavior is harmful to the project or its people. Decisions take into account the severity of the
behavior and whether it was repeated after a warning.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers. Open a private report from the repository's
**Security** tab with **Report a vulnerability**
(<https://github.com/mineragpu/minera/security/advisories/new>), and start the title with
"Conduct:" so it is routed correctly. Include links, screenshots and the names of the people
involved if you can.

Every report is reviewed promptly and kept confidential. The maintainers will not act against
anyone for making a report in good faith.

## Scope

This code applies in every project space, and in public spaces when someone is representing the
project.
